Planning-use disclaimer

This tool displays public GIS records and performs proximity-based network analysis. It does not establish legal ownership, pipe invert elevations, hydraulic capacity, actual flow direction, maintenance responsibility, flood risk, or engineering sufficiency. Missing GIS geometry does not prove an asset is absent. Field survey, City records and engineering analysis should control design, permitting, maintenance and construction decisions.
