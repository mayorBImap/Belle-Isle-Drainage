# GIS Layer Reference

## Orange County AGOL_Open_Data2
Service: https://ocgis4.ocfl.net/arcgis/rest/services/AGOL_Open_Data2/MapServer

- 2 — Stormwater Drainwell
- 3 — Stormwater Major Control Structure
- 4 — Stormwater Pollution Control Devices
- 5 — Stormwater Structures
- 6 — Stormwater Ditch
- 7 — Stormwater Pipes
- 8 — Stormwater Ponds
- 10 — Jurisdiction Dissolved (used to select Belle Isle)

Orange County describes this public service as updated weekly and supporting GeoJSON queries.

## Orange County iWorQ
Service: https://ocgis4.ocfl.net/arcgis/rest/services/iWorQ/MapServer

- 4 — Primary Canals
- 5 — Primary Canal Segments
- 7 — Stormwater Pump Stations
- 9 — Control Structures
- 10 — Stormwater Ponds
- 11 — Secondary Canals
- 12 — Culverts
- 13 — Pond Structures
- 14 — Major Drainage Structures

The iWorQ culvert layer is described as Orange County culverts maintained by Orange County Public Works Department — Stormwater Division. It is therefore not assumed to be a complete inventory of all City/private culverts.

## Orange County AGOL_Open_Data
Service: https://ocgis4.ocfl.net/arcgis/rest/services/AGOL_Open_Data/MapServer

- 28 — Hydrology
- 33 — Major Drainage Basins

Hydrology includes natural and man-made water bodies. Major Drainage Basins depicts the County's major surface drainage basins.
