## Orange County AGOL_Open_Data
Service: https://ocgis4.ocfl.net/arcgis/rest/services/AGOL_Open_Data/MapServer

- 28 — Hydrology
- 33 — Major Drainage Basins

Hydrology includes natural and man-made water bodies. Major Drainage Basins depicts the County's major surface drainage basins.


## Belle Isle + Edgewood scope
Municipal boundaries are requested from the Orange County jurisdiction layer in AGOL Open Data2 and used client-side to clip infrastructure to the combined planning area. Lake Conway is a shared waterbody spanning Belle Isle, Edgewood and unincorporated Orange County.

## Trimble Park documented planning feature
Belle Isle planning/capital documents identify a **Lagoon/Trimble Park Outfall Repair** and a **Trimble Park drainage improvement**. Version 5 shows Trimble Park as a documented planning location. It does not assert the exact outfall coordinate, pipe invert, hydraulic direction or discharge rate.


## Jade Storm Pump Station — Belle Isle
City of Belle Isle RFP #2024-06 identifies the Jade Storm Pump Station project location as **5274 Jade Circle, Belle Isle, FL 32812**. City agenda materials state a new 80 kW generator was selected to power the station's **two pumps**, and later Public Works reporting noted the generator installation. Version 6 shows the station as a named City-documented planning asset. Exact surveyed coordinates, discharge direction, invert elevations, and hydraulic capacity remain engineering/as-built verification items.
